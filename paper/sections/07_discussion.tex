\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Long-context retrieval alone leaves source-document accounting errors unresolved. Distractors hurt, and controlled-evidence records still degrade as cross-document accounting dependencies grow because models must select evidence, form balanced entries, and aggregate them. Repair ablations target the entry-to-balance-sheet aggregation stage. The larger gain from ledger feedback implicates aggregation errors beyond insufficient sampling. Strict document-grounded accuracy remains low. A targeted causal test is needed to explain the reduction in inconsistency detection.

\paragraph{Intended utility.}
Beyond fixed evaluation, the released generator can create stratified practice data and organization-specific stress tests, while the deterministic ledger can provide machine-checkable feedback for training or candidate selection. Possible uses include supervised examples, verifier/reward signals, and distillation of tool-assisted workflows.
