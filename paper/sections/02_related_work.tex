\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Financial-NLP question answering.}
FinQA \citep{chen-etal-2021-finqa} and ConvFinQA \citep{chen-etal-2022-convfinqa} pose numerical questions over single financial reports and their tabular content, with the latter requiring conversational multi-turn reasoning. TAT-QA \citep{zhu-etal-2021-tatqa} similarly evaluates models on hybrid tabular-and-text questions over filings. DocFinQA \citep{reddy-etal-2024-docfinqa} extends this paradigm to longer financial documents, and MultiHiertt \citep{zhao-etal-2022-multihiertt} targets reasoning over multiple hierarchical tables and accompanying text. These benchmarks assume that a prepared structured artifact, such as a table extracted from a filing or a single report, already exists and ask the model to answer questions \emph{over} it. \benchmark{} instead requires the model to construct a statement from a bundle of raw source documents with OCR text.

\paragraph{Financial benchmark suites.}
FinanceBench \citep{islam2023financebench}, FinanceMATH \citep{zhao-etal-2024-financemath}, FinBen \citep{xie2024finben}, FinDABench \citep{liu-etal-2025-findabench}, FinTagging \citep{wang2025fintagging}, FinRule-Bench \citep{malarkkan2026finrulebench}, FinMaster \citep{jiang2025finmaster}, and FinAuditing \citep{wang2026finauditing} broaden financial evaluation to filings, math, tagging, taxonomy-structured auditing, accounting principles, and workflow-style tasks (\tabref{tab:prior-work-comparison}). Most evaluate answers, tags, judgments, or reasoning over prepared artifacts. FinMaster comes closest by simulating transaction-to-statement workflows and including statement-generation and auditing tasks, but its simulator omits a general ledger and trial balance, while the benchmark stops short of a citation-grounded ledger and balance sheet reconstructed from raw source-document bundles. \benchmark{} fills this gap through computed ledger ground truth that makes consistency between journal entries and their aggregated balance sheet directly measurable.

\paragraph{Inference-time diagnostics.}
Self-consistency decoding \citep{wang2023selfconsistency}, refinement methods \citep{madaan2023selfrefine,shinn2023reflexion}, trained verifiers \citep{cobbe2021gsm8k,lightman2024verify}, retrieval augmentation \citep{lewis2020rag}, long-context analyses \citep{liu2024lostmiddle}, and tool-use agents \citep{yao2023react} motivate our diagnostics. Our diagnostics use the benchmark ledger, evidence-only visibility, context stress, and tool conditions to localize failures in this accounting task.
